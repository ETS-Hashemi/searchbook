Must cover: the communication graph of a swarm (undirected/directed, neighbours within radius R_comm), adjacency, degree and LAPLACIAN matrices with their properties (from ch:appB); the CONSENSUS protocol xdot_i = sum_{j in N_i} (x_j - x_i), i.e. xdot = -L x, the theorem: if the graph is connected the states converge to the average of the initial states, with the proof via the eigen-decomposition (zero eigenvalue with the all-ones eigenvector; convergence rate given by the algebraic connectivity lambda_2); discrete-time consensus x_{k+1} = (I - eps L) x_k with the step-size condition eps < 1/d_max; directed graphs and spanning trees in one paragraph (Ren & Beard); LEADER-FOLLOWER consensus (one agent pinned to a reference, e.g. the CBS path); FORMATION CONTROL by displacement: xdot_i = sum_j (x_j - x_i - d_ij) where d_ij are the desired relative positions; convergence to the formation up to translation (proof from consensus on the shifted variables), with a figure of four drones converging into a square; the FORMATION ERROR metric used in ch:ch25 (defined precisely, e.g. RMS of ||p_i - p_j - d_ij|| over edges, or after removing the best translation); distance-based formation in one paragraph (rigidity pointer) and why displacement-based is the simpler choice with a shared frame; second-order (double-integrator) consensus with velocity damping, since drones are double integrators; FLOCKING (Olfati-Saber): the three terms - gradient-based separation/cohesion with a smooth potential, velocity alignment (consensus on velocities), and navigation feedback toward a target - and Reynolds' boids as the origin; the connection to potential fields (ch:ch15) and to ORCA (ch:ch13) as the safety term; COMMUNICATION-RANGE constraints: connectivity maintenance (keep every edge shorter than R_comm) as a constraint or as a potential that becomes infinite at the range boundary; lambda_2 as a connectivity measure to monitor; how this becomes a constraint in MPC (ch:ch21); a worked example with numbers from the code (4 drones, a given graph, the Laplacian written out, the eigenvalues, the convergence time constant 1/lambda_2); generated figures: consensus convergence of 6 agents on two graphs with different lambda_2 (pgfplots), a formation forming while following a leader along a path, and a plot of formation error and min/max edge length over time under an avoidance manoeuvre; implementation notes (discrete step size, asynchronous updates, packet loss, switching topologies); pitfalls (unstable step size, unconnected graph -> clusters, formation vectors inconsistent (d_ij != -d_ji or non-closing cycles), forgetting that avoidance breaks the formation temporarily); drone box (formation preservation and communication radius as the constraints of the capstone: consensus as the low-level formation controller, lambda_2 and the edge lengths as the monitored constraints, and what happens during avoidance); 8 exercises incl. a coding exercise (simulate formation control with a communication radius and measure formation error during an avoidance manoeuvre) and a proof exercise on convergence.
