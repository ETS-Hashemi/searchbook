Source: the capstone section of docs/core-idea.txt: four layers - (1) global swarm planner CBS/ECBS planning nominal time-indexed paths and resolving conflicts among connected swarm members before execution; (2) prediction layer Kalman/LSTM observing an unknown external drone and predicting a short future trajectory with uncertainty; (3) local safety layer ORCA or DWA producing a temporary safe velocity when a predicted collision enters a short safety horizon, without immediately discarding the global route; (4) replanning layer D* Lite or A* from the current state when the local manoeuvre cannot safely return to the nominal route, followed by a re-check of swarm conflicts and formation/communication constraints. Decision logic: (i) follow the nominal CBS/ECBS path while no predicted conflict is inside the safety horizon; (ii) if an unknown moving object creates a near-term risk, invoke ORCA/DWA; (iii) after the conflict clears, attempt to reconnect to the nominal path at a safe future waypoint; (iv) if reconnection is impossible, too costly, or violates formation/communication constraints, run D* Lite/A* from the current state; (v) re-check inter-agent conflicts after any significant replan.
