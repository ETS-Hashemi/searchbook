Must cover: why optimal CBS does not scale and what a bound buys; FOCAL search in general (OPEN ordered by f, FOCAL = {n in OPEN : f(n) <= w * f_min}, ordered by a secondary heuristic h_FOCAL that need not be admissible; the returned solution is within w of optimal), with the theorem and proof; focal search at the LOW level (h_FOCAL = number of conflicts the path has with the other agents' current paths, so the low level prefers paths that create fewer conflicts while staying within w of that agent's optimal cost) and at the HIGH level (FOCAL over CT nodes with h_FOCAL = number of conflicts in the node's solution; the lower bound LB = min over OPEN of the sum of per-agent lower bounds); the bookkeeping of lower bounds that makes the w-suboptimality proof work (each low-level search returns both a path and a lower bound on that agent's cost under its constraints); full ECBS pseudocode; a worked example contrasting CBS and ECBS on the same instance (same figure family as ch:ch09; table with cost, CT nodes, and the bound); the theorem: ECBS returns a solution of cost <= w * C* (proof); choosing w; a generated benchmark figure: runtime and solution cost vs number of agents for CBS and ECBS with w = 1.1, 1.5, 2.0 (pgfplots from .dat), plus a table of the speed/quality trade-off; EECBS in one section (explicit estimation search + online learned lower bounds, concepts only); implementation notes (two heaps kept consistent, updating FOCAL when f_min rises, tie-breaking); pitfalls (using an inadmissible h in OPEN, forgetting to update the lower bound, w applied per agent vs to the sum); drone box (why a bounded-suboptimal global planner is the practical choice for a swarm and how the bound interacts with the safety layer); 8 exercises incl. the Week-5 coding exercise (add ECBS, benchmark against CBS) and a proof exercise on the bound.
