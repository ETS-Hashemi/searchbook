% Glossary terms from ch10: lines of the form \item[Term] Definition.
\item[Bounded-suboptimal algorithm] An algorithm with a parameter $w\ge1$ that returns, on every solvable instance, a solution of cost at most $w\,C^*$ without computing the optimum $C^*$; $w$ is the suboptimality factor.
\item[Focal search ($\astar_\eps$)] Best-first search that keeps \Open ordered by an admissible $\fcost=\gcost+\hcost$ and expands, among the open nodes with $\fcost\le w\,\fcost_{\min}$, the one preferred by a secondary heuristic; its solution costs at most $w\,C^*$ whatever the secondary heuristic is.
\item[FOCAL list] The band $\set{n\in\Open:\fcost(n)\le w\,\fcost_{\min}}$ of a focal search, kept as a second heap ordered by the secondary heuristic and refilled from \Open whenever $\fcost_{\min}$ rises.
\item[Secondary heuristic $\hcost_{\Focal}$] The function that chooses the node inside the focal band; it need not be admissible or even estimate cost. In \ecbs it is the number of conflicts.
\item[Conflict heuristic $h_c$] The number of vertex and swapping conflicts in the paths of a CT node, or of a partial low-level path with the other agents' current paths; the secondary heuristic of \ecbs at both levels.
\item[Per-agent lower bound $\mathrm{LB}_i(N)$] A number stored in a CT node that is at most the cost of the cheapest path of agent $a_i$ under the node's constraints; the focal low level returns it as the $\fcost_{\min}$ of its \Open when the goal is popped.
\item[Global lower bound $\mathrm{LB}$] The smallest $\mathrm{LB}(N)=\sum_i\mathrm{LB}_i(N)$ over the open CT nodes; it never exceeds $C^*$, so a conflict-free node with $\cost\le w\,\mathrm{LB}$ is $w$-suboptimal, and $\cost/\mathrm{LB}$ is the bound proven by a run.
\item[ECBS] Enhanced conflict-based search: \cbs with focal search at the low level (conflict-avoiding paths within $w$ of the agent's optimum, returned with a lower bound) and at the high level (\Open by $\mathrm{LB}(N)$, \Focal by conflicts); its solutions cost at most $w\,C^*$.
\item[BCBS$(w_H,w_L)$] Bounded \cbs: focal search with factor $w_L$ at the low level and $w_H$ at the high level, the latter measured against the smallest cost in \Open instead of a lower bound; guarantee $w_Hw_L\,C^*$ only.
\item[Explicit estimation search (EES)] A bounded-suboptimal search that orders nodes by a lower bound, by an inadmissible estimate of the final solution cost, and by a distance-to-go estimate, and chooses among the three fronts while keeping every expanded solution within $w$ of the lower bound.
\item[EECBS] \ecbs with explicit estimation search at the high level, estimates learned online during the search, and the \cbs improvements (bypass, cardinal conflicts, symmetry reasoning, high-level heuristics); keeps the bound $w$ and scales to hundreds of agents.
