% Glossary terms from ch02: lines of the form \item[Term] Definition.
\item[Adjacency list] A representation of a graph that stores, for every vertex, the list of its neighbours together with the edge weights.
\item[Configuration space] The set of all positions (configurations) of a robot; it splits into the free part $\Cfree$ and the obstacle part $\Cobs$.
\item[Corner cutting] A diagonal move on an 8-connected grid that passes the corner of an obstacle cell; the book forbids it because the swept disc of the robot would clip the obstacle.
\item[Covariance ellipse] The set of points at a fixed Mahalanobis distance from the mean of a Gaussian; its axes are the eigenvectors of the covariance and its semi-axes are the square roots of the eigenvalues.
\item[Double integrator] The motion model in which the control input is the acceleration, so that the state consists of position and velocity.
\item[Lazy deletion] The priority-queue idiom of pushing a duplicate entry instead of decreasing a key, and skipping entries that are stale when they are popped.
\item[Makespan] The number of time steps until the last agent of a plan has reached its goal for good.
\item[Minkowski sum] The set of all sums $\vect{a}+\vect{b}$ with $\vect{a}$ in one set and $\vect{b}$ in another; inflating an obstacle by the robot's radius is a Minkowski sum with a disc.
\item[Space-time state] A pair $(v,t)$ of a vertex and a discrete time index; the states of the time-expanded graph.
\item[Sum of costs] The total number of time steps, summed over all agents, until each agent has reached its goal for good.
\item[Time-expanded graph] The graph whose vertices are the space-time states and whose edges are the move and wait actions between consecutive time layers.
\item[Time of closest approach] The time at which two objects moving with constant velocities are closest to each other.
\item[Trajectory] A time-parametrised curve in continuous time, represented in the book by waypoints with velocities.
\item[Wait action] The action that keeps an agent at its current vertex for one time step.
