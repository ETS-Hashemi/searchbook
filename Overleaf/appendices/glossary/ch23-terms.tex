% Glossary terms from ch23: lines of the form \item[Term] Definition.
\item[Communication graph] The graph $G=(V,E)$ whose vertices are the drones and whose edges join pairs that can exchange messages; for a radio of range $R_{\mathrm{comm}}$ it is the radius graph with $\{i,j\}\in E$ iff $\norm{\pos_i-\pos_j}\le R_{\mathrm{comm}}$, and it changes as the drones move.
\item[Graph Laplacian] $\mat{L}=\mat{D}-\mat{A}$, the degree matrix minus the adjacency matrix; symmetric, positive semidefinite, with $\mat{L}\vect{1}=\vect{0}$ and $\vect{x}\T\mat{L}\vect{x}=\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E}(x_i-x_j)^2$.
\item[Algebraic connectivity] $\lambda_2(\mat{L})$, the second-smallest Laplacian eigenvalue (Fiedler value); positive if and only if the graph is connected, and the exponential rate at which consensus is reached.
\item[Consensus protocol] $\dot{\vect{x}}_i=\sum_{j\in N_i}(\vect{x}_j-\vect{x}_i)$, i.e.\ $\dot{\vect{x}}=-\mat{L}\vect{x}$; on a connected undirected graph every state converges to the average of the initial states.
\item[Disagreement vector] $\vect{\delta}=\vect{x}-\bar x\vect{1}$, the distance of the states from agreement; it decays like $e^{-\lambda_2 t}$ under the consensus protocol.
\item[Discrete-time consensus] $\vect{x}_{k+1}=(\mat{I}-\eps\mat{L})\vect{x}_k$, the sampled version of the protocol; it converges for $0<\eps<2/\lambda_n$, and $\eps<1/d_{\max}$ is the sufficient bound every drone can check locally.
\item[Leader-follower (pinned) consensus] Consensus in which some drones add a term $b_i(\vect{r}-\vect{x}_i)$ pulling them to an external reference $\vect{r}$; with a connected graph and at least one pinned drone all states converge to $\vect{r}$.
\item[Displacement-based formation control] $\dot{\pos}_i=\sum_{j\in N_i}(\pos_j-\pos_i-\vect{d}_{ij})$ with desired relative positions $\vect{d}_{ij}=\vect{o}_j-\vect{o}_i$; it is consensus on the shifted variables $\pos_i-\vect{o}_i$ and converges to the formation up to a translation.
\item[Formation error] $e_F=\sqrt{\frac{1}{\abs{E_F}}\sum_{\{i,j\}\in E_F}\norm{\pos_j-\pos_i-\vect{d}_{ij}}^2}$, the RMS violation of the desired displacements over the edges of the formation graph; translation invariant and zero exactly on the formation.
\item[Distance-based formation control] Formation control that prescribes only inter-drone distances, needs no common frame, defines the shape up to rotation and reflection, and requires a rigid formation graph.
\item[Second-order consensus] Consensus for double integrators, $\ddot{\pos}_i=\sum_j a_{ij}[k_p(\pos_j-\pos_i-\vect{d}_{ij})+k_v(\vel_j-\vel_i)]-k_d\vel_i$; the velocity terms provide the damping without which the swarm oscillates for ever.
\item[Flocking] Olfati-Saber's controller with a gradient term (separation and cohesion from a smooth pairwise potential), velocity alignment (consensus on velocities) and navigation feedback towards a target; the formal descendant of Reynolds' boids.
\item[Connectivity maintenance] Keeping the communication graph connected while the drones move, by constraining the edges of a spanning subgraph to stay shorter than $R_{\mathrm{comm}}$ or by a potential that becomes infinite at the range boundary; monitored through $\lambda_2$.
\item[Switching topology] A communication graph that changes with time (drones move, packets are lost); consensus still converges if the union of the graphs over bounded time windows is connected.
