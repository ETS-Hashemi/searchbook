% Glossary terms from ch22: lines of the form \item[Term] Definition.
\item[Linear program (LP)] Minimization of a linear objective subject to linear equalities, inequalities and bounds; its feasible set is a polyhedron and its optimum is attained at a vertex.
\item[Polyhedron] The intersection of finitely many half-spaces; convex, with flat faces and vertices, the feasible set of every LP.
\item[Mixed-integer linear program (MILP)] An LP in which some variables are restricted to integers; with binary variables it encodes choices, either-or conditions, switches and assignments.
\item[LP relaxation] The LP obtained from a MILP by dropping the integrality requirements; its optimal value is a bound on the integer optimum.
\item[Big-$M$ constraint] An inequality $f(x) \le M b$ with a binary $b$ and a constant $M$ at least as large as the largest possible violation, so that $b = 1$ switches the inequality off and $b = 0$ keeps it on.
\item[Branch-and-bound] The exact MILP search that solves LP relaxations, branches on a fractional integer variable and discards subproblems whose bound cannot beat the incumbent.
\item[Incumbent] The best solution with integral values found so far during branch-and-bound; every node whose bound is not better is pruned.
\item[MIP gap] The relative difference between the incumbent's cost and the best remaining bound; zero certifies optimality, and a time-limited solve reports the gap it reached.
\item[Branch-and-cut] Branch-and-bound strengthened by cutting planes, valid inequalities that cut off fractional relaxation optima, plus presolve and primal heuristics, as in HiGHS.
\item[Minimum-fuel objective] The total velocity change $\dt \sum_k \norm{\ctrl_k}_1$ of a trajectory, linearized with one auxiliary variable per input component.
\item[Time-expanded flow formulation] The integer program of Yu and LaValle for MAPF: one unit of flow per agent on the time-expanded graph, with capacity-one states and anti-swap rows.
\item[Corner cutting] The violation of an obstacle or separation constraint between two sampled instants at which it holds; prevented by inflating obstacles by $v_{\max}\dt/2$ and separations by $v_{\max}\dt$.
\item[Symmetry breaking] An extra constraint that removes mirror-image solutions of equal cost from a MILP, such as fixing on which side one drone passes another, so that branch-and-bound explores half the tree.
