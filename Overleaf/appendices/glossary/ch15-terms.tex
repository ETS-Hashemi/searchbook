% Glossary terms from ch15: lines of the form \item[Term] Definition.
\item[Artificial potential field] A scalar function $U(\pos)$ over the free space, low at the goal and high near obstacles, whose negative gradient $-\nabla U$ is used as the velocity (or force) command of a robot.
\item[Attractive potential] The goal term $U_{\mathrm{att}}=\tfrac12 k_{\mathrm{att}}\norm{\pos-\pos_{\mathrm{g}}}^2$ (quadratic) or $k_{\mathrm{att}}d^*\norm{\pos-\pos_{\mathrm{g}}}-\tfrac12 k_{\mathrm{att}}(d^*)^2$ beyond the switch distance $d^*$ (conic); the hybrid of the two keeps the force bounded far away and smooth at the goal.
\item[Repulsive potential] Khatib's obstacle term $U_{\mathrm{rep}}=\tfrac12 k_{\mathrm{rep}}(1/\rho-1/\rho_0)^2$ for $\rho\le\rho_0$ and $0$ otherwise, where $\rho$ is the clearance to the obstacle; its force $k_{\mathrm{rep}}(1/\rho-1/\rho_0)\rho^{-2}\nabla\rho$ grows without bound as $\rho\to0$.
\item[Clearance] The distance $\rho(\pos)$ from the robot position to the nearest point of an obstacle; $\nabla\rho$ is the unit vector pointing away from that point.
\item[Influence distance] The radius $\rho_0$ beyond which an obstacle exerts no repulsion; the repulsive potential and its gradient both vanish at $\rho=\rho_0$, so $U$ is continuously differentiable.
\item[Local minimum (of a potential field)] A point other than the goal where the attractive and repulsive forces cancel and the potential rises in every direction; a gradient-descent robot that enters its basin stops there.
\item[GNRON] Goals non-reachable with obstacles nearby: when the goal lies inside the influence region of an obstacle, the repulsion at the goal is non-zero and the minimum of $U$ moves away from the goal; the standard fix multiplies the repulsive term by $\norm{\pos-\pos_{\mathrm{g}}}^n$.
\item[Stiffness] The largest eigenvalue $\lambda_{\max}$ of the Hessian of $U$ at a point; the explicit update $\pos\gets\pos+\dt\,\vect{F}$ is stable near a minimum only if $\dt\,\lambda_{\max}<2$, and stiffness grows steeply in narrow passages.
\item[Navigation function] A potential on a bounded free space with a unique minimum at the goal, whose other critical points are non-degenerate saddles; Rimon and Koditschek construct one explicitly for sphere worlds.
\item[Harmonic potential] A potential satisfying Laplace's equation $\nabla^2U=0$ in the free space; by the maximum principle it has no interior local minima, but it must be computed globally on a grid.
\item[Inter-agent repulsion] A pairwise repulsive term between drones with clearance $\rho_{ij}=\norm{\pos_i-\pos_j}-2r$, the potential-field form of Reynolds's separation rule.
\item[Flocking rules] Reynolds's separation, alignment and cohesion: repel close neighbours, match their average velocity, and move towards their centroid; alignment is a velocity consensus and cohesion plus separation a pairwise spacing potential.
