% Glossary terms from appB: lines of the form \item[Term] Definition.
\item[Positive semidefinite matrix] A symmetric matrix $\mat{A}$ with $\vect{x}\T\mat{A}\vect{x}\ge0$ for all $\vect{x}$, equivalently with no negative eigenvalue, written $\mat{A}\succeq\mat{0}$; every covariance matrix is one, and $\mat{A}\succ\mat{0}$ (positive definite) additionally excludes zero eigenvalues.
\item[Schur complement] For a block matrix $\begin{bsmallmatrix}\mat{A}&\mat{B}\\\mat{C}&\mat{D}\end{bsmallmatrix}$ with invertible $\mat{D}$, the matrix $\mat{A}-\mat{B}\mat{D}\inv\mat{C}$; it is the top-left block of the inverse's inverse, gives the block determinant, and is the covariance of a conditional Gaussian.
\item[Woodbury identity] The matrix inversion lemma $(\mat{A}+\mat{U}\mat{C}\mat{V})\inv=\mat{A}\inv-\mat{A}\inv\mat{U}(\mat{C}\inv+\mat{V}\mat{A}\inv\mat{U})\inv\mat{V}\mat{A}\inv$, which trades a large inverse for a small one and links the covariance and information forms of the Kalman update.
\item[Normal equations] The linear system $\mat{A}\T\mat{A}\vect{x}=\mat{A}\T\vect{b}$ whose solution minimises $\norm{\mat{A}\vect{x}-\vect{b}}^2$; unique when $\mat{A}$ has full column rank.
\item[Jacobian] The matrix $\partial g_i/\partial x_j$ of first derivatives of a vector function, one row per output; it is the linear map that best approximates the function near a point, with an error of second order in the displacement.
\item[Mahalanobis distance] $d(\state)=\sqrt{(\state-\vect{\mu})\T\mat{\Sigma}\inv(\state-\vect{\mu})}$, the distance from the mean measured in units of the covariance; for a Gaussian vector $d^2$ is $\chi^2_n$ distributed, which is the basis of gating.
\item[Conditional Gaussian] For jointly Gaussian $(\state_a,\state_b)$, the distribution $\state_a\mid\state_b\sim\Normal(\vect{\mu}_a+\mat{\Sigma}_{ab}\mat{\Sigma}_{bb}\inv(\state_b-\vect{\mu}_b),\,\mat{\Sigma}_{aa}-\mat{\Sigma}_{ab}\mat{\Sigma}_{bb}\inv\mat{\Sigma}_{ba})$; the Kalman update is this formula applied to a state and a linear measurement of it.
\item[Convex function] A function whose graph lies below every chord, $f(\theta\vect{x}+(1-\theta)\vect{y})\le\theta f(\vect{x})+(1-\theta)f(\vect{y})$; every local minimum of a convex function on a convex set is global, and a twice differentiable function is convex iff its Hessian is positive semidefinite everywhere.
\item[KKT conditions] Stationarity of the Lagrangian, primal and dual feasibility and complementary slackness; necessary for a local minimiser under a constraint qualification and sufficient for convex problems, with the multipliers measuring the cost of tightening each active constraint.
\item[LP relaxation] The linear program obtained from a mixed-integer program by dropping the integrality constraints; its optimal value bounds the integer optimum, which branch and bound exploits by splitting on a fractional variable and pruning subproblems whose bound cannot beat the incumbent.
\item[Big-M constraint] The pair $\vect{a}\T\vect{z}\le\beta+Mb$, $b\in\set{0,1}$, with $M$ a large constant, which enforces a linear constraint when $b=0$ and switches it off when $b=1$; with a sum constraint on the binaries it expresses ``at least one of these constraints holds''.
\item[Algebraic connectivity] The second-smallest eigenvalue $\lambda_2$ of the graph Laplacian $\mat{L}=\mat{D}-\mat{A}$; it is positive iff the graph is connected and sets the convergence rate of consensus, while $\lambda_n\le2d_{\max}$ bounds the safe step size.
\item[Big-O notation] $f(n)=\bigO{g(n)}$ when $f(n)\le c\,g(n)$ for all $n$ beyond some $n_0$: an upper bound up to a constant factor, with $\Omega$ the matching lower bound and $\Theta$ both at once; a bound must always be read in the right input variables.
\item[Binary heap] A complete binary tree stored in an array, in which no key is larger than its children's, so the minimum is at the root; insertion and extraction of the minimum sift one key along a root-to-leaf path and cost $\bigO{\log n}$, and building a heap from $n$ unsorted items costs $\bigO{n}$.
\item[Hash table] A container that maps a key to an array position with a hash function and resolves collisions by probing or chaining, so insertion, lookup and deletion take constant expected time; \python's \code{dict} and \code{set} are hash tables and require immutable keys.
\item[Multivariate Gaussian] The density $\Normal(\vect{\mu},\mat{\Sigma})$ whose exponent is $-\tfrac12$ times the squared Mahalanobis distance from the mean; it stays Gaussian under affine maps, under the addition of independent Gaussian noise, and under marginalisation and conditioning, which is what makes the Kalman filter a closed-form recursion.
\item[Bayes' rule] $p(\state\mid\meas)\propto p(\meas\mid\state)\,p(\state)$: the posterior is the likelihood times the prior, normalised by $p(\meas)$; applied after the law of total probability it gives the predict--update recursion that every filter of the book runs.
