% Glossary terms from ch19: lines of the form \item[Term] Definition.
\item[Coordinated-turn model] The motion model with state $(x, y, v, \psi, \omega)$, constant speed $v$ and constant turn rate $\omega$, whose position update follows a circular arc of radius $v/\abs{\omega}$; nonlinear through $v\cos\psi$, $v\sin\psi$ and the division by $\omega$.
\item[Range--bearing measurement] The pair $(r, \varphi)$ of distance and direction from a sensor to the intruder, $r = \norm{(x, y) - \vect{s}}$ and $\varphi = \operatorname{atan2}(y - s_y, x - s_x)$; a bearing error of $\sigma_\varphi$ radians displaces the position by about $r\sigma_\varphi$ across the line of sight.
\item[Angle wrapping] Mapping an angle to $(-\pi, \pi]$ with $\operatorname{wrap}(\theta) = \theta - 2\pi\lceil(\theta - \pi)/(2\pi)\rceil$; applied to every difference of angles in an innovation, a residual or a likelihood, and to the heading after every update.
\item[Extended Kalman filter (EKF)] The Kalman filter applied to the tangents of $f$ at the previous estimate and of $h$ at the prediction, with the Jacobians $\mat{F}_k$ and $\mat{H}_k$ in place of the linear model matrices; exact for linear models, biased when the functions curve across the belief.
\item[Linearization error] The difference between the Gaussian produced by the tangent of a nonlinear function and the true (non-Gaussian, ``banana'') distribution of its output; it grows with the curvature of the function times the width of the belief.
\item[Unscented transform] The approximation of the mean, covariance and cross-covariance of $g(\state)$ obtained by mapping $2n+1$ sigma points through $g$ and combining the images with the weights $W^{(m)}$ and $W^{(c)}$; exact for affine $g$ and second-order accurate in the mean for any smooth $g$.
\item[Sigma points] The $2n+1$ points $\bar{\state}$ and $\bar{\state} \pm \vect{l}_i$, where $\vect{l}_i$ are the columns of a square root of $(n+\lambda)\mat{P}$ with $\lambda = \alpha^2(n+\kappa) - n$; their weighted mean and covariance equal $\bar{\state}$ and $\mat{P}$ exactly.
\item[Unscented Kalman filter (UKF)] The filter that replaces both linearizations of the EKF by unscented transforms and uses the cross-covariance between state and predicted measurement in place of $\mat{P}\mat{H}\T$; it needs no Jacobians.
\item[Particle filter] A filter that represents the belief by $N$ weighted samples, propagates them through the motion model with sampled noise, weights them by the measurement likelihood and resamples when the weights degenerate; it can represent multimodal and non-Gaussian beliefs at a cost linear in $N$.
\item[Bootstrap filter] The particle filter that uses the motion model as its proposal distribution, so that each weight is multiplied by the likelihood $p(\meas_k \mid \state_k^{(i)})$ of the new measurement; also called sequential importance resampling (SIR).
\item[Importance weight] The factor that corrects a sample drawn from a proposal distribution so that the weighted set represents the target distribution; in the bootstrap filter it is proportional to the accumulated measurement likelihoods.
\item[Effective sample size] $N_{\mathrm{eff}} = 1/\sum_i (w^{(i)})^2$, a number between $1$ and $N$ that measures how evenly the weight is spread; resampling is triggered when it falls below a threshold such as $N/2$.
\item[Systematic resampling] Resampling with one uniform draw $u \in [0, 1/N)$ and the grid $u + (j-1)/N$ over the cumulative weights, which copies each particle $\lfloor Nw^{(i)}\rfloor$ or $\lceil Nw^{(i)}\rceil$ times in $\bigO{N}$ time.
\item[Degeneracy] The state of a particle filter in which a few particles carry almost all the weight after repeated weighting without resampling.
\item[Sample impoverishment] The loss of diversity after resampling, when the surviving particles are copies of a few ancestors that the process noise has not yet separated; aggravated by resampling at every step and by small process noise.
