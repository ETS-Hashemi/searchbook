% Glossary terms from ch14: lines of the form \item[Term] Definition.
\item[Dynamic window] The set $V_d$ of velocities a robot can reach from its current velocity within one control interval under its acceleration limits; DWA searches only inside it.
\item[Admissible velocity] A velocity from which the robot can still stop before the nearest obstacle on its rollout: $v\le\sqrt{2\,\dist\,a_b}$, or $v\dt+v^2/(2a_b)\le\dist$ when the command is held for one interval before braking starts.
\item[Braking distance] The distance $v^2/(2a_b)$ covered while decelerating from speed $v$ at the constant rate $a_b$ to a standstill.
\item[Velocity space] The space of velocity commands, $(v,\omega)$ for a differential drive and $(v_x,v_y,v_z)$ for a velocity-controlled drone, in which DWA searches.
\item[Rollout] The trajectory a candidate command would produce if held constant: a circular arc of radius $v/\omega$ for a differential drive, a straight segment for a drone; its free distance is the clearance of the candidate.
\item[Free distance] The distance along a rollout to the first obstacle inflated by the robot radius, capped at the sensing range $d_{\max}$.
\item[Heading term] $1-\theta/\pi$, where $\theta$ is the angle between the candidate velocity and the direction to the target seen from the predicted position; it rewards progress towards the goal or a lookahead point.
\item[Clearance term] The free distance of the rollout divided by $d_{\max}$; it rewards open space ahead of the robot.
\item[Velocity term] The candidate speed divided by $v_{\max}$; it rewards fast motion.
\item[Braking candidate] The command that keeps the current direction and reduces the speed by $a_b\dt$; it always lies in the window and is admissible whenever the previous command was, so DWA never runs out of safe options with static obstacles.
\item[Lookahead point] The point at a fixed arc length ahead of the robot's projection on a global path, used as the target of the heading term to keep a local planner out of local minima.
\item[Hysteresis (DWA)] Keeping the current command unless the best candidate beats its score by a margin $\eta$; it damps the oscillation between two nearly equal commands but does not remove a trap.
\item[Local minimum (DWA)] A state from which the greedy, memoryless rule keeps the robot at rest or shuttling although a collision-free path to the goal exists; the reason why DWA is not complete.
\item[Differential drive] A wheeled robot commanded by a translational speed $v$ and a rotational speed $\omega$; with both constant it moves on a circular arc of radius $v/\omega$.
