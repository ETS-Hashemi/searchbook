% Hints for the coding exercise(s) of ch25 (assembled into Appendix C)

\begin{solution}{exr:ch25-coding}
Checkpoints. \code{ch25\_\allowbreak evaluation.py} is built as the chapter asks you to build yours:
\code{make\_\allowbreak scenario} turns a seed and a cell into a scenario, \code{run\_\allowbreak episode}
returns a log and nothing else, \code{compute\_\allowbreak metrics} turns one log into the ten
numbers of \cref{tab:ch25-metrics}, \code{run\_\allowbreak study} loops over cells, strategies and
seeds, and \code{summarise}, \code{paired\_\allowbreak table}, \code{wilson}, \code{mean\_\allowbreak ci} and
\code{holm} do the statistics. Keep one flat table of raw rows, one row per run, carrying
cell, strategy, seed and every metric; write it to disk and never aggregate inside the
simulator. Steps: fix the seed contract first, so that the same seed gives the same
scenario for \emph{every} strategy and \cref{thm:ch25-pairing} applies; run the sanity
baseline before anything else, since it must collide when an intruder is aimed at a drone
and never when $m=0$; budget the run, knowing that the four-cell mini-study is $320$ runs
in about $15$~s, so the one-factor-at-a-time design is minutes rather than hours;
aggregate by script from the raw rows, with Wilson intervals for rates and $t$ or
bootstrap intervals for means; write the results section, limitations included. Expected
numbers on the chapter's cells: with $m=0$ all four strategies produce identical rows
(path ratio $0.999$, makespan $13.2$), which is a test and not a result; with $k=4$,
$m=1$ the sanity baseline collides on $75\,\%$ of seeds, \code{local} and \code{hybrid}
on $5\,\%$ and \code{replan} on none, at path ratios $1.03$, $1.05$ and $1.08$ and means
of $0$, $1.5$ and $7.5$ replans. The paired comparison carries the conclusion: hybrid
against replan-only differs by $-5.95$ replans $[-7.59,-4.31]$ and by $-0.71$~m of
minimum separation, while hybrid against local-only differs on path ratio by $+0.021$
$[+0.006,+0.036]$, a real but tiny effect. Split the sanity baseline's collisions by
intruder family ($14/14$ straight, $14/14$ turning, $4/12$ evasive) before concluding
anything about difficulty. Classic mistakes: reporting success rate only; unpaired
comparisons over paired seeds; a mean path ratio taken over runs that ended in a
collision; one $p$-value per metric per cell with no correction. Done when the matrix
runs unattended, every row is reproducible from its seed, and the box, cactus and Pareto
figures and the table come out of the raw rows with no manual step --- the Week~12
milestone of \cref{ch:appA}. Part~(d) tests the interface: if swapping the stand-ins for
your real modules touches anything but \code{run\_\allowbreak episode}, the seam was in the wrong
place.
\end{solution}
