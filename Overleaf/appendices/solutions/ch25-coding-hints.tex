% Hints for the coding exercise(s) of ch25 (assembled into Appendix C)

\begin{solution}{exr:ch25-coding}
Checkpoints. \code{ch25\_\allowbreak evaluation.py} is built as the chapter asks you to build yours:
\code{make\_\allowbreak scenario} turns a seed and a cell into a scenario, \code{run\_\allowbreak episode} returns
a log and nothing else, \code{compute\_\allowbreak metrics} turns one log into the ten numbers of
\cref{tab:ch25-metrics}, \code{run\_\allowbreak study} loops over cells, strategies and seeds, and
\code{summarize}, \code{wilson} and \code{holm} aggregate. Keep one flat table of raw
rows, one per run, carrying cell, strategy, seed and every metric; write it to disk and
never aggregate inside the simulator. Steps: fix the seed contract first, so that the same
seed gives the same scenario for \emph{every} strategy and \cref{thm:ch25-pairing}
applies; run the sanity baseline before anything else, since it must collide when an
intruder is aimed at a drone and never when $m=0$; budget the run, knowing that the
four-cell mini-study is $320$ runs in about $15$~s, so the one-factor-at-a-time design is
minutes rather than hours; aggregate by script, with Wilson intervals for rates and $t$
intervals for means. Expected numbers: with $m=0$ all four strategies produce identical
rows (path ratio $0.999$, makespan $13.2$), which is a test and not a result; with $k=4$,
$m=1$ the sanity baseline collides on $75\,\%$ of seeds, \code{local} and \code{hybrid} on
$5\,\%$ and \code{replan} on none, at path ratios $1.03$, $1.05$ and $1.08$ and means of
$0$, $1.5$ and $7.5$ replans. The paired comparison carries the conclusion: hybrid against
replan-only differs by $-5.95$ replans $[-7.59,-4.31]$ and $-0.71$~m of minimum
separation, and against local-only by $+0.021$ $[+0.006,+0.036]$ of path ratio, a real but
tiny effect. Classic mistakes: reporting success rate only; unpaired comparisons over
paired seeds; a mean path ratio taken over runs that ended in a collision; one $p$-value
per cell with no correction. Done when the matrix runs unattended, every row is
reproducible from its seed, and the figures and the table come out of the raw rows with no
manual step --- the Week~12 milestone of \cref{ch:appA}. Part~(d) tests the interface: if
swapping the stand-ins for your real modules touches anything but \code{run\_\allowbreak episode}, the
seam was in the wrong place.
\end{solution}
