% Hints for the coding exercise(s) of ch14 (assembled into Appendix C)

\begin{solution}{exr:ch14-coding}
Checkpoints. In \code{ch14\_\allowbreak dwa.py} start from \code{window\_\allowbreak candidates} (the grid over
the window of \cref{def:ch14-window}, with the braking candidate always appended),
\code{free\_\allowbreak distance} along a rollout, \code{evaluate} (the three normalized terms of
\cref{eq:ch14-G}) and \code{dwa\_\allowbreak command}; \code{simulate} closes the loop and three of
the four failure scenes are already built. Obstacles are disks and boxes with closed-form
ray tests, the state is a position and a velocity vector (the drone of
\cref{def:ch14-drone} is holonomic, so there is no heading), and one \code{DwaParams}
record holds $v_{\max}$, $a_{\max}$, $\dt$, the horizon, the grid step $\Delta v$ and the
three weights. Steps: write \code{free\_\allowbreak distance} and test it alone --- a wall $1$~m ahead
at $2$~m/s must be a collision within three steps; add the admissibility filter of
\cref{eq:ch14-vadm} \emph{before} scoring; normalize the three terms over the admissible
set, then score; only then build the failures and their repairs. Expected numbers: in an
open field the drone arrives in about $17$ steps over $6.2$~m; the U-trap is \emph{not}
solved --- the drone stalls against the back wall near $(4.7,0.0)$ --- and a global \astar
path repairs it in $25$ steps and $10.2$~m; the crossing intruder collides under a static
rollout and is cleared in $18$ steps by the relative-velocity rollout. In 3D the candidate
count is $(2a_{\max}\dt/\Delta v+1)^3$: $125$ candidates and about $1.7$~ms per step at
$\Delta v=0.25$~m/s against $1\,331$ and $17$~ms at $0.1$~m/s --- the whole argument for a
coarse grid plus a global path. Classic mistakes: summing the three terms before
normalizing, so the units choose the trajectory; skipping the braking check; sampling the
window without intersecting it with $\norm{\vel}\le v_{\max}$. Done when each failure has
a figure, a number that explains it and one remedy that fixes it, and you own a small
tuning table of the weights --- the Week~7 milestone of \cref{ch:appA}.
\end{solution}
