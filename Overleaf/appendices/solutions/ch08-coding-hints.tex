% Hints for the coding exercise(s) of ch08 (assembled into Appendix C)

\begin{solution}{exr:ch08-coding}
Checkpoints. Four objects of \code{ch08\_\allowbreak prioritized.py} carry the exercise: the class
\code{ReservationTable}, the search \code{space\_\allowbreak time\_\allowbreak astar} (Cooperative \astar,
\cref{alg:ch08-ca}), the loop \code{prioritized\_\allowbreak planning} (\cref{alg:ch08-pp}) and
\code{validate}. The table of \cref{def:ch08-reservation} is three dictionaries, not one:
occupied cells keyed by $(v,t)$, occupied moves keyed by $(u,v,t)$, and a last-reserved
time per cell that is $\infty$ for a parked agent. The low-level state is $(v,t)$ with a
wait action and the true-distance heuristic of \cref{ch:ch03}. Steps: build the table and
its three queries first and unit-test them on hand-written paths; add the goal-stay test
of line~\ref{alg:ch08-ca:goal}, so the search may return only when the goal is free from
$t$ on; loop over the agents, plan, reserve, and report failure as a value; add
well-formedness and restarts last. Expected numbers: on \cref{ex:ch08-three} four of six
orders succeed, at $\sumcost=16$ or $21$; on the pocket corridor every order fails
although the optimum is $11$ (\cref{thm:ch08-incomplete}); on the bypass corridor every
order returns $16$ against $15$ (\cref{thm:ch08-suboptimal}). On the $20\times20$ grids of
\cref{fig:ch08-orders} a random order succeeds on about $97\,\%$ of instances at $k=16$
and $87\,\%$ at $k=32$, while longest-first and five restarts stay near $100\,\%$. Classic
mistakes: reserving vertices but not moves, so swaps slip through; letting an arrived
agent free its goal; returning at the first pop of the goal; a Manhattan heuristic in a
cluttered map, which costs about three times the expansions of the true-distance table.
Done when your validator passes every plan, well-formed instances always solve, and you
own one instance that one order fails and another solves --- the Week~3 milestone of
\cref{ch:appA}.
\end{solution}
