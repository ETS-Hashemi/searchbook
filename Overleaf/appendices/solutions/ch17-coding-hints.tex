% Hints for the coding exercise(s) of ch17 (assembled into Appendix C)

\begin{solution}{exr:ch17-coding}
Checkpoints; read \code{ch17\_\allowbreak rrt\_\allowbreak star.py} only when stuck, but copy its shape.
\code{StarTree} keeps five parallel arrays --- positions, parents, edge lengths, costs
and children lists --- and that last one is the trick: without children you cannot
propagate a rewire, and a tree whose costs silently drift is the classic bug of this
chapter, which is why \code{costs\_\allowbreak consistent} exists.
\code{connection\_\allowbreak radius} implements \cref{eq:ch17-radius} with the cap $\eta$,
\code{knn\_\allowbreak count} the variant of \cref{eq:ch17-knn}, \code{InformedSampler} the transform
of \cref{eq:ch17-transform}. Steps: add costs to your \rrt of \cref{ch:ch16} and check
them against a walk to the root; add \textsc{Near} and \textsc{ChooseParent}, sorting the
candidates by $\mathrm{Cost}(\vect{v})+\norm{\state_{\mathrm{new}}-\vect{v}}$ and
collision-checking in that order, stopping at the first free one; add \textsc{Rewire}
with subtree propagation; add informed sampling last; write the self-test of~(d) before
the experiments. Expected numbers: on the hand example the nearest vertex is
$\vect{v}_4$ at $0.943$, the sorted candidate costs are $4.578$ (blocked), $5.264$,
$5.440$, $5.692$, $7.436$, so the parent is $\vect{v}_7$ at $5.264$, and the rewire
lowers $\vect{v}_8$ from $6.451$ to $6.249$ and $\vect{v}_9$ from $7.583$ to $7.380$. On
the 2D map with seed~1 the first solution costs $21.00$ at iteration $351$ and improves
to $19.73$, $18.21$, $17.45$ and $17.32$ at $500$, $1\,000$, $2\,000$ and $3\,000$
iterations, against an optimum of $16.91$ and a string-pulled grid path of $17.23$ ---
inside the $5\,\%$ the self-test asks --- while plain \rrt returns $25.72$ and never
improves. In the 3D field with $\eta=1.5$ expect $23.5$ near iteration $260$ and about
$21.1$ after $1\,500$, still some $20\,\%$ above the $17.27$ that grid \astar at
$h=0.25$~m reaches in under two seconds: in three dimensions the sample budget must grow
a lot before \rrtstar catches the grid, and that is the honest conclusion. Informed
sampling helps where the ellipsoid shrinks: on the wide map it takes $16.77$ to $14.03$.
Classic mistakes: rewiring without updating descendants; a radius that never shrinks
because the cap is applied the wrong way round; a $\gamma$ from a free-volume estimate
below the true $\mu(\Xfree)$; comparing a raw \rrtstar path with a string-pulled grid
path. Done when the self-test passes and your convergence plot and grid table support
the one-paragraph rule of the Week~8 milestone (\cref{ch:appA}).
\end{solution}
