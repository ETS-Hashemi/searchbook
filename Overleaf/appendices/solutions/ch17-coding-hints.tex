% Hints for the coding exercise(s) of ch17 (assembled into Appendix C)

\begin{solution}{exr:ch17-coding}
Checkpoints; read \code{ch17\_\allowbreak rrt\_\allowbreak star.py} only when stuck, but copy its shape.
\code{StarTree} keeps five parallel arrays --- positions, parents, edge lengths, costs and
children lists --- and that last one is the trick: without children you cannot propagate a
rewire, and a tree whose costs silently drift is the classic bug of this chapter.
\code{connection\_\allowbreak radius} implements \cref{eq:ch17-radius} and \code{InformedSampler} the
transform of \cref{eq:ch17-transform}. Steps: add costs to your \rrt of \cref{ch:ch16} and
check them against a walk to the root; add \textsc{Near} and \textsc{ChooseParent},
sorting the candidates by $\mathrm{Cost}(\vect{v})+\norm{\state_{\mathrm{new}}-\vect{v}}$
and collision-checking in that order, stopping at the first free one; add \textsc{Rewire}
with subtree propagation; add informed sampling last; write the self-test of~(d) before
the experiments. Expected numbers: on the hand example the sorted candidate costs are
$4.578$ (blocked), $5.264$, $5.440$, $5.692$, $7.436$, so the parent is $\vect{v}_7$ at
$5.264$, and the rewire lowers $\vect{v}_8$ from $6.451$ to $6.249$ and $\vect{v}_9$ from
$7.583$ to $7.380$. On the 2D map with seed~1 the first solution costs $21.00$ at
iteration $351$ and improves to $18.21$ and $17.32$ at $1\,000$ and $3\,000$ iterations,
against an optimum of $16.91$ and a string-pulled grid path of $17.23$ --- inside the
$5\,\%$ the self-test asks --- while plain \rrt returns $25.72$ and never improves. In the
3D field with $\eta=1.5$ expect about $21.1$ after $1\,500$ iterations, still some
$20\,\%$ above the $17.27$ that grid \astar at $h=0.25$~m reaches in under two seconds;
informed sampling helps only where the ellipsoid shrinks. Classic mistakes: rewiring
without updating descendants; a radius that never shrinks because the cap is applied the
wrong way round; comparing a raw \rrtstar path with a string-pulled grid path. Done when
the self-test passes and your convergence plot and grid table support the one-paragraph
rule of the Week~8 milestone (\cref{ch:appA}).
\end{solution}
