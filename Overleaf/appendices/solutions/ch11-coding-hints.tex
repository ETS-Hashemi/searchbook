\begin{solution}{exr:ch11-coding}
Checkpoints for an optional extension: the study plan asks only that you know what \mstar
and Push-and-Swap do. The reference is \code{ch11\_\allowbreak mstar.py}: \code{joint\_\allowbreak astar} with its
operator-decomposition flag, \code{independence\_\allowbreak detection}, \code{mstar} with
\code{limited\_\allowbreak neighbours}, and the \code{PushSwapState} primitives. A joint state is a
tuple of $k$ cells, so it hashes and keys a dictionary directly; the individual policies
of \cref{def:ch11-policy} are one backward breadth-first search per agent, computed once
and shared; a collision set is a set of agent indices stored \emph{per joint state}.
Steps: write the joint successor function and the cost of \cref{eq:ch11-cost} first, with
a parked agent paying nothing, and test two agents against your \cbs of \cref{ch:ch09};
add the heuristic of \cref{eq:ch11-heuristic}; for~(b) merge two groups only when
re-planning one around the other at \emph{equal} cost fails; for~(c) reverse a completed
swap by replaying the recorded moves \emph{by position}, not by agent. Expected numbers:
on the chapter's worked example joint \astar expands $24$ states and generates $612$,
operator decomposition $100/292$, independence detection $27/162$ and \mstar $31/127$, all
four at cost $15$. On random $8\times8$ maps (\cref{fig:ch11-expansions}) the mean largest
group stays near $2.8$ at $k=6$ while joint \astar generates over a hundred thousand
states against about a thousand for independence detection at the median. Push-and-Swap
solves the swap example in $14$ moves at $\sumcost=27$ where the optimum is a makespan of
$7$ and $\sumcost=14$ (\cref{thm:ch11-par}). Classic mistakes: a collision set stored per
agent instead of per state; back-propagating collisions without re-opening the parent; a
swap that leaves everyone but the two agents displaced. Done when every plan validates,
your solvers agree with \code{joint\_\allowbreak astar} on a few dozen random instances, and you can
say why these methods lose on \cref{ex:ch11-aisles}.
\end{solution}
