% Hints for the coding exercise(s) of ch16 (assembled into Appendix C)

\begin{solution}{exr:ch16-coding}
Checkpoints. The pieces exist: \code{scene\_\allowbreak 3d} builds the box field, \code{rrt} and
\code{rrt\_\allowbreak connect} plan in it, \code{shortcut} and \code{prune} smooth
(\cref{alg:ch16-shortcut}), \code{path\_\allowbreak is\_\allowbreak free} re-checks a path at ten times the
resolution, and \code{grid\_\allowbreak astar\_\allowbreak reference} in \code{ch17\_\allowbreak rrt\_\allowbreak star.py} is a ready
26-connected grid \astar with string pulling. Keep the tree as three parallel arrays
(positions, parents, count) rather than linked objects, and the world as arrays of box
corners so that a whole segment sample is tested at once. Steps: fix the benchmark
protocol \emph{before} the first run, with the same $30$ start--goal pairs, the same seeds
and one row per (query, planner); report path length relative to the best path any method
found on that query, never against the straight line; give every planner the same budget
and smooth every path, grid paths included. Expected numbers on the chapter's
$10\times10\times10$ field (straight line $15.59$~m): \rrt solves near iteration $320$
with about $160$ vertices at length $24.5$, which shortcutting brings to $20.4$;
\rrt-Connect solves near iteration $120$ with $59$ vertices. Grid \astar gives $19.90$ at
$h=1$~m ($1\,331$ cells), $17.44$ at $0.5$~m and $17.27$ at $0.25$~m ($68\,921$ cells,
$1.6$~s): on a small map the grid wins on quality and the sampler on memory. The last part
makes the crossover visible --- $200\times200\times60$~m at $0.25$~m is $1.5\times10^{8}$
cells, which no \python grid search will hold, while the samplers do not notice. Classic
mistakes: checking the new vertex but not the segment to it; a linear-scan nearest
neighbor ($0.03$~ms at a thousand vertices, $2.9$~ms at a hundred thousand, called every
iteration); averaging over queries one planner failed. Done when your length-against-time
plot carries all three methods and one paragraph says, with your numbers, when you would
use graph search and when sampling --- the Week~8 milestone of \cref{ch:appA}.
\end{solution}
