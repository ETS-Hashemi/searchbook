\chapter{About the author}
\markboth{About the author}{About the author}

\noindent
\textbf{Seyed Masoud Hashemi Ahmadi} is a PhD candidate in the Department of Systems
Engineering at École de technologie supérieure (ÉTS) in Montréal, where he works under
the supervision of Professor Julio Montecinos. His research addresses the automated
planning of trajectories for a swarm of drones: flying a fleet through shared airspace
while keeping its formation, avoiding collisions among its own members, and avoiding
obstacles, both fixed ones and moving ones such as drones that do not belong to the
swarm.

\medskip\noindent
The algorithms taught in this book are the ones he gathered and studied for that
problem, from graph search and multi-agent path finding to reactive collision
avoidance, tracking, trajectory prediction and control. The hybrid architecture of
\cref{ch:ch24} is the system design he developed to solve it, and the two together form
the framework used in the implementation of the research project. Every algorithm comes
with its pseudocode and a \python{} implementation so that readers can start from
working code.
